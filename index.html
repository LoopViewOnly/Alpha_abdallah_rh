The Art of Vanilla Notes in Fragrance

Vanilla is far more than a simple sweet scent—it is the ultimate anchor of luxury evening fragrances. In cold night air, vanilla creates an opulent warm aura that projects effortlessly through winter layers.

Why Vanilla Works in Winter

Unlike citrus or light florals, vanilla’s dense molecular structure resists rapid evaporation in freezing air. Blended with spices like cinnamon and fruits like apple—as in Afnan 9PM—it crafts a cozy, irresistible sillage.
