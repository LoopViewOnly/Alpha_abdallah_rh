01 — INTRODUCTION

What Is Oud?

Oud, also known as agarwood, is a rare and precious aromatic material obtained from the resin-rich heartwood of Aquilaria trees. Its distinctive fragrance has made it highly valued in perfumery for centuries.

02 — THE ORIGIN

Nature Creates Luxury

Oud develops when certain Aquilaria trees respond to fungal infection or other forms of stress by producing a dark, fragrant resin. This natural process can take years, contributing to the rarity and value of high-quality agarwood.

Natural Oud vs. Synthetic Oud

Natural oud oil can be extremely expensive. Modern perfumes may also use sustainably sourced ingredients or carefully designed synthetic accords to recreate aspects of oud's complex aroma.
